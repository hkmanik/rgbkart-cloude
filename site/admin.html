Payment requests

★ Featured listing requests

Call the seller to collect the payment, then press Payment received: start. Featured runs for the plan's days, then ends by itself. One-time sellers' phone, WhatsApp and email are shown to buyers only while Featured is on.

Advertisements

Everything about ads in one place: create an ad (yours or for a seller, paid or free), set the ad spaces and prices per day, and check sellers' ad payments. An ad goes live on its dates once it is paid or made free.

📣 All advertisements

💳 Payments for advertisements

Sellers pay by bKash / Nagad / Upay / Rocket and send the TrxID. Check your app: if the money arrived press Verify & start and the ad goes live on its dates. To publish a seller's ad without payment, use Publish free on the Advertisements tab.

Ad spaces, picture sizes & prices

Price per day for each ad space. The ad price is worked out as price per day × number of days (you can still change it on each ad).

Reviews

Star ratings and reviews from buyers whose order was delivered. Hide a review that is rude, fake, has phone numbers or is about something else. A hidden review stays saved but stops counting in the stars.

Pages & policies

About, Contact, Terms, Privacy, Return & refund and Seller rules, shown in the footer of every page in English and Bangla. They start as drafts: fill in the parts in [square brackets] and have a lawyer check Terms and Privacy before launch. Format: "## " for a heading, "- " for a bullet, "1. " for a numbered step, an empty line between paragraphs, **bold**.

Social media links

Icons on the right of the footer on every page. Paste the full address (starting with https://). An icon without a link shows faded and can't be clicked.

Only the Master Admin and Co-admins can change these links.

Verification

Shops send their NID or trade licence to get the "✓ Verified shop" badge. Check that the name matches the shop owner and the photo is clear and real, then approve or reject with a note. Documents are private.

Activity log

Everything the RGBKart team changed, and who did it: approvals, rejections, payments, Featured, categories, home page, menu, settings, hidden reviews and more. Entries are written by the database automatically and cannot be edited or deleted. Only the Master Admin and Co-admins can see this page.

Returns

All return requests on RGBKart. Shops handle their own returns; step in if a shop does not answer or a customer complains (you can accept, decline or mark received for any shop).

Questions

Buyers' questions on product pages and the sellers' answers. Hide a question or answer that is rude, spam or shares contact details it should not.

Complaints

Reports from shoppers about products and shops (fake items, scams, no delivery…). Check each one, take action where needed (reject the product in Products, suspend the shop in Sellers, hide a review), then mark it resolved or dismissed. The person who reported gets an alert with your note.

Orders

All orders on RGBKart. Each shop confirms, packs and delivers its own orders and collects the cash. You can step in and cancel an order if needed.

OrderShopCustomerItemsTotalStatus

Delivery methods

The delivery methods shops can offer. Each shop ticks the ones it offers in Seller Center → Delivery and sets its own fee up to the maximum here (empty fee = your suggested fee). Shops that have not set this up use the normal methods at your suggested fees. "For" makes a special method for Heavy, Vehicle, Flammable or Fragile products: those products can only use a matching method, pickup or "agree with the seller".

Cash on Delivery limits

Protects shops from fake or very large Cash on Delivery orders. Checked by the database for every new order. Write 0 for no limit.

Cancellation rules

Until which step a buyer can cancel an order by themselves. After that they can only ask the shop, and the shop accepts or declines. Shops must always give a reason when they cancel.

VAT on invoices

Prices already include VAT. When this is on, each new order records how much of each item's price is VAT, and the shop's invoice and the buyer's order show it. Set rates per category (Categories → edit) or per product (Products → % VAT); the default rate below is used when neither has one. Orders placed earlier don't change.

SMS messages

Short text messages to buyers and shops about orders, sent through BulkSMSBD. Each message costs SMS credit from your BulkSMSBD account (Bangla messages count about double). Keep SMS off until real buyers and shops use the site; you can still send a test message while it is off.

Payments

Sellers pay Featured and ad fees to your numbers below, then send the Transaction ID (TrxID). Check your bKash / Nagad / Upay / Rocket app: if the money arrived, press Verify and it starts at once. Each TrxID can be used only once.

Featured listing rates
